# ICE Investigative Case Management / Investigative Analytics

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** ICE ICM/IA is a current, deeply integrated investigative data/case system supplied by Palantir. That establishes technical and organizational Material Participation, but the system itself supports a broad law-enforcement mission and is not proven by procurement records alone to be primarily or systematically deployed for an ECL 0.2 prohibited use.

## 2. Exact project identity and boundary

January 2026 SAM.gov notice **ICE_JA-25-0266** describes sole-source procurement to Palantir Technologies for modernization of ICE's **Investigative Case Management (ICM)** and **Investigative Analytics (IA)** systems.

ICE describes ICM as the official system of record for investigative cases, used to manage case/subject records, connect records across systems and conduct telephone/address and investigative searches.

## 3. ECL criteria tested

Potential project fit includes:

- §5.1 if subject-data/search functionality is materially used for persecution, political repression, arbitrary detention or suppression of dissent;
- §5.6 if the deployment is materially used in a systematic arbitrary-detention or forced-transfer/expulsion project meeting the exact clause; and
- §5.7 if used to circumvent an incorporated restriction.

Ordinary criminal investigation, immigration administration or data quality is not prohibited merely because the system can support enforcement.

## 4. Material participants and roles

- U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement / Homeland Security Investigations — operator/customer.
- Palantir Technologies / relevant contracting entity — software supplier, modernization, licensing, O&M/support and custom enhancements according to procurement records.

This proves technical participation but not prohibited intent/output by itself.

## 5. Evidence supporting continued review

The 2026 procurement states that ICM is integral to ICE operations and supports investigative case management, subject-record linking and target address/telephone searches. Earlier official procurement records identify the customized Gotham platform as the existing ICM solution.

The canonical U.S. State dossier separately identifies current federal immigration/detention projects as potentially qualifying where project-level evidence establishes arbitrary detention, forced transfer without meaningful protection or other ECL criteria.

## 6. Counter-evidence / alternative explanation

The procurement describes a general investigative-case-management system with domestic/transnational criminal-investigation purposes. Palantir states that its ICE work is intended to improve data quality and reduce erroneous enforcement. No procurement text reviewed proves that ICM/IA's primary purpose or known material function is an ECL prohibited use.

## 7. Attribution and exclusions

This dossier does not classify all ICE activity or all Palantir government work. Any future restriction should identify the exact ICM/IA workflow, module, data integration or downstream enforcement project shown to produce qualifying conduct.

## 8. Adversarial determination

**RETAIN `U`.** Sensitive capability + material supply is insufficient. Escalation requires project-specific proof connecting ICM/IA to qualifying surveillance, arbitrary detention, forced transfer, persecution or other ECL 0.2 conduct.

## 9. Review / removal triggers

Escalate on authoritative technical/operational evidence of systematic prohibited deployment. Move toward `N` if current review establishes effective safeguards and no prohibited-use linkage despite substantial evidence access.
