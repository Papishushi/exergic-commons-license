# Intellexa / Predator commercial spyware network

> **Governance record only.** `Intellexa Consortium` is a network/marketing label, not a substitute for exact legal-entity identification in a Schedule.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization/network restriction candidate.** Authoritative U.S. Treasury records directly identify multiple legal entities as developing, holding, reselling, distributing or financially enabling Predator commercial spyware. A future Schedule must list exact entities rather than designate an amorphous `consortium`.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

Current authoritative records identify at least:

- **Intellexa S.A.** — Greece-based software development company within the network;
- **Intellexa Limited** — Ireland-based reseller/asset-holding entity;
- **Cytrox AD** — North Macedonia-based Predator developer;
- **Cytrox Holdings ZRT** — Hungary-based entity that previously developed Predator;
- **Thalestris Limited** — Ireland-based entity holding Predator distribution rights / financial-holding role; and
- **Aliada Group Inc.** — British Virgin Islands-based network/financial enabler identified by Treasury in September 2024.

The next Schedule should use those exact names and should not infer additional entities solely from branding or remote affiliation.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.1 Repressive surveillance** — Predator is an invasive spyware platform capable of covert device access, data extraction, geolocation and microphone/message access; official records describe targeting of journalists, policy experts, opposition politicians and officials.
- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination/unlawful political repression** where spyware materially enables persecution or reprisal.
- **§5.7 Circumvention** for entities used deliberately to distribute/finance a restricted spyware project around an incorporated restriction.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

In March 2024 U.S. Treasury described the Intellexa Consortium as a network of offensive-cyber companies marketing Predator spyware, stated that the network enabled proliferation of commercial spyware to authoritarian regimes and that Predator was deployed to covertly surveil officials, journalists and policy experts. Treasury assigned concrete roles to Intellexa S.A., Intellexa Limited, Cytrox AD, Cytrox Holdings ZRT and Thalestris Limited.

In September 2024 Treasury further described Intellexa as a decentralized network whose past spyware targets included officials, journalists, policy experts and opposition politicians, and identified Aliada Group Inc. as a financial/network enabler.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

U.S. sanctions/export-control decisions do not automatically create ECL status. The ECL finding rests on the described product, entity roles and repressive-targeting use, not merely on sanctions presence.

The record does not justify treating every shareholder, employee, remote affiliate or unknown `Intellexa`-branded entity as Restricted.

## 6. Attribution, projects and exclusions

Scope follows the exact named entities **in their Predator/commercial-spyware development, ownership, resale, distribution, operation, transaction-processing or material technical-support capacities** where those activities materially enable qualifying repressive surveillance.

Unrelated activities and persons do not inherit status automatically. Exact corporate control and successor entities require fresh evidence.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD scoped `S` for the named network entities; reject an undefined blanket consortium class.** The direct product-role evidence supports entity/business-line restriction while ECL's knowability rule requires exact legal names.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Re-review on dissolution/restructuring, legal-name/control changes, independently verified cessation of Predator activity, durable safeguards preventing repressive deployment, or material contrary evidence.
